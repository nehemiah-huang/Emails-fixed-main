A DIFFERENT WAY TO WORK

Independent in your practice. Together in possibility.

An Ɛdan research affiliate is someone who chooses to base part of their practice within the space. It is a light, flexible affiliation for those who want to work alongside others while remaining independent in their projects and commitments.

A space to think, make and exchange.

Ɛdan Membership Interest Form

Ɛdan is a creative urban studio in Osu, Accra, bringing together people who work with and through the city.

Our research space and creative studio are open to researchers, creatives, spatial practitioners, collectives, students and partner institutions, whether you are based in Accra or passing through.

What our members share is a core aim of working towards a more fairer city, and we ask everyone who joins to bring that commitment into their work and into the space.

Initial introductory membership is 850 GHS a month for access Monday to Friday from 8am - 5pm for the artist studios and research space.
